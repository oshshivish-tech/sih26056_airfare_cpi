Problem Statement, Algorithmic Pipeline & Proposed Solution

MoSPI Airfare Index
The Problem (Current MoSPI Model)
  • • Manual Survey Bottleneck: Field surveyors manually visit OTAs once a month for single spot quotes.
  • • Blind to Dynamic Surges: Completely misses algorithmic yield swings (peak hours, holidays, load factors).
  • • Lead-Time Neglect: Fails to account for fare curves (1d emergency surge vs. 45d advance discounts).
  • • 15-Day Policy Lag: Lagging data impairs RBI monetary rate-setting responsiveness.
The Solution: AirIntel India
  • • Headless Web Harvester: Playwright cron jobs scraping Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet & OTAs nightly.
  • • 5 Lead-Time Horizons: Continuous sampling across 1, 7, 15, 30, and 45 days forward.
  • • UN/ILO Jevons Formula: Elementary geometric mean eliminates arithmetic substitution bias.
  • • DGCA Passenger Weighting: Weighted Laspeyres index calibrated to actual city-pair seat volume.
End-to-End Dataflow Pipeline
1. Scheduled Ingestion (02:00 AM IST)
Headless stealth scrapers harvest all non-stop flights across 12 corridors × 5 booking horizons.
2. Normalization & Z-Score Cleanse
Strips taxes/GST, standardizes flight numbers, and flags extreme surge anomalies (|Z| > 3.0).
3. Elementary Aggregation (Jevons GM)
Geometric mean computed per route × horizon to satisfy UN/ILO transitivity axioms.
4. DGCA Laspeyres Higher Aggregation
Corridor indices weighted by DGCA monthly seat share to yield National Daily Index.
5. SHA-256 Provenance & MoSPI Export
Immutable cryptographic audit manifest generated alongside interactive dashboard UI.

  • • Eliminates Upward Bias: Replaces Dutot arithmetic overestimation with Jevons geometric mean.

Value Proposition for Government

Mathematical Rigor & Index Formulas
1. Elementary Jevons Geometric Index UN/ILO Mandate
I_J = [ ∏_{i=1}^n (P_{i,t} / P_{i,0}) ]^(1/n) = exp( (1/n) ∑ ln(P_{i,t} / P_{i,0}) )
Neutralizes upward substitution bias; strictly satisfies multi-period transitivity.
2. DGCA Weighted Laspeyres National Aggregation National Index
I_{National, t} = ∑_{c=1}^{12} [ W_c × I_{c, t} ]   (where ∑ W_c = 1.0)
Weights (W_c) derived from DGCA Monthly Domestic City-Pair passenger volume reports.
3. Dynamic Z-Score Outlier Filter Surge Filter
Z_i = ( P_i - μ_c ) / σ_c   (|Z_i| > 3.00 flagged for review)
Isolates dynamic pricing shocks, festival spikes, and fare submission errors automatically.

Research Foundations, Official Literature & Standards

Academic & Policy Backing
1. UN / ILO Consumer Price Index Manual (2020)
• Global Standard: Jointly published by IMF, ILO, OECD, Eurostat, UNECE, and the World Bank.
• Jevons Mandate (Chapter 10): Strongly advocates geometric mean (Jevons) at the elementary aggregate level over arithmetic means (Dutot/Carli).
• Axiomatic Superiority: Satisfies the time reversal test and eliminates upward substitution bias caused by price volatility.
2. DGCA Domestic Air Traffic Reports (2025-2026)
• Official Traffic Volume: Directorate General of Civil Aviation, Government of India domestic city-pair data.
• Empirical Weighting: Corridor weights reflect actual passenger volume (e.g. DEL-BOM 18.5%, BOM-BLR 11.2%, DEL-BLR 10.8%).
• Representative Accuracy: Prevents low-traffic regional routes from skewing the national consumer price indicator.
3. MIT Billion Prices Project & Web Scraping in CPI
• Foundational Paper: Cavallo & Rigobon (2016), Journal of Economic Perspectives.
• Empirical Validation: Demonstrates that daily automated web scraping tracks official offline inflation statistics with zero latency.
• Global Adoption: Adopted by UK Office for National Statistics (ONS), Statistics Netherlands, and US BLS for airfares.
4. MoSPI / NSO Base Year (2012=100) Guidelines
• National Standard: Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation CPI Technical Manual.
• Laspeyres Integration: AirIntel indices directly scale to the 2012=100 base year and plug into higher-level CPI transport baskets.
• Open Data Standards: Provenance vault adheres to Government of India National Data Sharing & Accessibility Policy (NDSAP).
