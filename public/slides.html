AIRINTEL INDIA

Augmenting CPI through High-Frequency Algorithmic Web Scraping
PROBLEM
  • • Manual Survey Bottleneck: Field surveyors manually visit OTAs once a month for single spot quotes.
  • • Blind to Dynamic Surges: Completely misses algorithmic yield swings (peak hours, holidays, load factors).
  • • Lead-Time Neglect: Fails to account for fare curves (1d emergency surge vs. 45d advance discounts).
  • • 15-Day Policy Lag: Lagging data impairs RBI monetary rate-setting responsiveness.
MoSPI Current Baseline Limitation
1. Scheduled Harvest
2. Normalization
3. Z-Score Filter
4. Jevons GM
5. Laspeyres
6. Dashboard
Our solution
An automated high-frequency pipeline that:
  • • Scrapes raw fares across IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet & OTAs via scheduled Playwright crawlers.
  • • Samples 5 forward lead times (1d, 7d, 15d, 30d, 45d) for complete horizon capture.
  • • Applies automated Z-score outlier filtering (|Z| > 3.0) to flag dynamic surges.
  • • Calculates UN/ILO Jevons Elementary Geometric Mean to eliminate substitution bias.
  • • Aggregates National Laspeyres Index weighted by DGCA monthly passenger volume.
  • • Generates cryptographic SHA-256 provenance hashes for official MoSPI auditability.
Why different
  • • Dynamic Yield Sensitivity: Captures real-time dynamic yield algorithms, not single monthly static quotes.
  • • Zero Upward Bias: Mathematically proven Jevons GM eliminates upward substitution bias over Dutot.
  • • Cryptographic Auditability: SHA-256 vault guarantees legal & statistical reproducibility for MoSPI.
Key Value Proposition
  • • >95% Cost Reduction: Slashes recurring field survey costs across major Indian metro airports.
  • • Zero-Lag CPI: Empowers RBI MPC with real-time leading transportation inflation figures.
  • • Aviation Oversight: Equips DGCA with instant surge anomaly alerts to detect regional price gouging.
